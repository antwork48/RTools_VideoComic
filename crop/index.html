The crop box always keeps the aspect ratio of the output size, so every image comes out at exactly the same pixel resolution. Drag the box or its corners, or use the arrow keys (Shift for 10 px). Frame, shadow, and animation are optional (animation has two layers: the frame effect itself, and the in/out motion of the whole image); PNG export uses no animation. Exports are transparent outside the frame; MP4 fills it with the chosen screen color. Add canvas padding so shadows and motion are not clipped.
