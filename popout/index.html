Pop-Out Frame

Upload a character PNG with a transparent background (or drag a file onto the preview, or paste with Ctrl+V). Drag the preview to move the character and use the mouse wheel to scale it. The frame hides everything outside it, except above the Top cut line and in the optional Side zones, which is what makes the character break out of the frame. Dashed guides show those cut lines in the preview only; they are never exported. Exports are transparent; MP4 fills the background with the chosen screen color for keying. GIF supports only 256 colors and on/off transparency.
